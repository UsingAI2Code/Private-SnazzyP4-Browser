Settings are as follows (defaults change once your browser cache updates):

<b>Role (Support or DPS)</b>
  <ul><li>This affects which marker shows for your Forked Lightning and Compressed Water debuffs. Defaults to Support</li></ul>
<b>Display Mode (Simple or Classic)</b>
  <ul><li>Simple does not take into account the long or short timers of your debuffs. It separates them into their own Debuff box since the player will mentally track <i>when</i> their timer will go off. Classic takes these into account and will display the Debuffs in First or Second Set. Defaults to Simple</li></ul>
<b>Column Views (Two Columns or One Column)</b> <i>!! Only available on Desktop Browsers !!</i>
  <ul><li>Two Columns separates the solver so Resolutions will show on the second column, making it easier to see. One Column, is well, just one column. Defaults to Two Columns</li></ul>
<b>Hide Exdeath/Chaos when Complete</b>
  <ul><li>When you complete the Exdeath and Chaos Debuffs, they will hide from view so only Kefka and Resolutions will show. This is only for the One Column view as it can be difficult to see the Resolutions on Desktop or Mobile browsers. Defaults to unchecked</li></ul>
